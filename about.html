Our Story

Risk analytics redefined

Founded with a vision to enable organisations across Africa to navigate complexity with confidence, Edge Analytics has grown into a trusted digital transformation partner. We specialize in converting institutional data into clear, actionable intelligence, bridging the gap between strategic design and technology implementation.

Whether auditing legacy architectures, developing predictive credit risk models, implementing NIST-aligned cybersecurity protocols, or building modern digital campuses for higher education, our dedication remains clear: delivering real outcomes for real business impact.

"Our mission is to empower organisations across Africa to make confident, data-driven decisions that shape a secure, resilient, and prosperous future."

WHAT WE DO

Solutions built for complex environments

From strategy design to ongoing operations, we deliver integrated service lines engineered to accelerate your organization's capability.

Digital services

  • Cloud Modernisation

    Migrating legacy systems and infrastructure to secure, scalable public and hybrid cloud environments.

  • Workflow Automation

    Streamlining operations, eliminating manual handoffs, and automating processes to drive efficiency.

  • Data, Analytics & AI

    Turning complex datasets into clear, interactive dashboards and building intelligent ML models.

  • Student Information Systems

    Unifying administrative workflows, enrollment tracking, and student records for TVET colleges and universities.

Risk management

  • Cybersecurity Resilience

    Protecting assets through NIST-aligned threat modeling, penetration auditing, and zero-trust perimeter defense.

  • Fraud & AML Compliance

    Deploying machine learning models for real-time transaction surveillance, anomaly detection, and compliance screening.

  • Credit Risk Analytics

    Developing predictive analytics engines, scorecard calibrations, and credit portfolio stress-testing dashboards.

  • Enterprise Risk & Governance

    Aligning corporate operations with international risk standards, compliance audits, and mitigation strategies.

Advisory services

  • IT & Digital Strategy

    Formulating actionable, phased transformation blueprints that align tech investments with business goals.

  • Enterprise Architecture

    Designing decoupled, robust, and scalable integrations to bridge legacy infrastructure with modern cloud applications.

  • Data Strategy & Governance

    Structuring institutional metadata catalogs, tracking lineages, and setting data standards aligned to POPIA and GDPR.

  • Digital Governance & Compliance

    Establishing robust policy frameworks, security governance protocols, and corporate compliance controls.

Managed services

  • Data Backup & Recovery

    Securing business continuity via automated offsite database backups and rapid replication protocols.

  • Cloud Operations (Cloud Ops)

    Delivering 24/7 cloud infrastructure monitoring, performance optimization, and server scaling.

  • Security Operations (SOC)

    Continuous network monitoring, incident response logging, and active firewall threat mitigation.

  • Helpdesk & Technical Support

    SLA-driven corporate support desk, hardware troubleshooting, and ongoing system maintenance.
